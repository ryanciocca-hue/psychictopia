The Free-Minutes Trap

Here’s how it works everywhere else.

You’re anxious. It’s late. You want an answer. You find a site offering 3 free minutes — or 5, or 10 if you’re lucky. You sign up, pick someone from a wall of faces, and start typing.

So you either hang up with nothing, or you spend $60 finding out whether this person is any good.

That’s not a trial. That’s a hook.

Chat gives you a transcript you can reread later, which matters more than people expect. Phone moves faster and feels more personal. California Psychics offers both; pick whichever you’d be more honest on.
