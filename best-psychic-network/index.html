Why the Free Minutes Rarely Give You What You Came For

You’ve probably done this before.

You find a site offering three free minutes. You sit down with your heart in your throat, and you start to explain. And explaining takes time, because the situation is layered, and the important part is never the first part.

By the time you’ve reached the thing that actually matters, the free minutes are gone. And now every sentence carries a price, so you rush. You skip the pause where you would have taken a breath. You give the short version instead of the true one.

A reading isn’t a transaction. It’s a connection between two people, and connection doesn’t open on command. It opens the way anything alive opens, which is to say slowly, and only once it feels safe.

Three minutes isn’t a reading. It’s an introduction that gets interrupted.

What you deserve is enough room to say the whole thing out loud, and then enough quiet afterward to hear what comes back.

FAQ

Is the welcome offer only for the first time?

Yes. It’s given once, on your first reading. Afterward, each advisor’s own rate applies, beginning around $5 a minute and rising with their experience.

Voice or chat, which should I choose?

Chat leaves you something to hold onto, and many people find themselves rereading it for days. Voice tends to open people faster, because it’s harder to guard yourself out loud. Choose whichever one you’d be most honest in.

What if I don’t feel a connection with the advisor I chose?

It happens, and it isn’t a failure on anyone’s part. End the session whenever you like, since you’re only charged for the minutes used, and your remaining balance stays yours to spend with someone else. Between that and the guarantee, one mismatch doesn’t cost you your welcome.

How long does a reading usually need to be?

Most people find the truth arrives somewhere between the tenth and twentieth minute. The first few are for arriving. Which is exactly why the welcome is twenty and not three.

What if I don’t know what to ask?

Most people don’t, not really. Bring the feeling instead of the question. A good advisor will find the question inside it.

Is anyone available right now?

Yes. Whatever hour it is where you are, someone is there. The advisor page will show you who.
